About the app

ChordWise Pro is a chord and lyrics app made for musicians who play live. Write or import your songs, transpose them to any key, line them up in a setlist, and run the whole show in Gig Mode with big, easy-to-read chords. It also comes with a tuner, so you can tune up without opening another app.

How it works

  

    
Write or import
Type your chords and lyrics, or import songs from a file.

    
Match your key
Tap ♯ or ♭ until it fits your singer.

    
Play in Gig Mode
Add songs to your setlist and tap to move to the next one.

  






  
Features

  

    
📚 Song Library
Keep all your songs in one place with a quick search bar to find any song fast.

    
📋 My Setlist
Pick songs from your library and build your setlist for the gig, church, or practice.

    
🚀 Gig Mode
Full-screen chords with tap-to-navigate between songs. Change key on the fly with ♯ and ♭ buttons.

    
🎼 Key Transpose
Move any song up or down with ♯ UP and ♭ DOWN so it always fits your singer.

    
🎸 Guitar & Bass Tuner
Built-in tuner with reference tones, different tunings, and adjustable A4 pitch.

    
📤 Export & Import
Back up your library as a file and import it again anytime, or share songs with bandmates.

    
🌙 Dark & Light Mode
Switch themes depending on the stage, the room, or your eyes.

    
⚡ Fast Typing Shortcuts
Mark song sections quickly, like verse or chorus, using simple shortcuts while you type.
